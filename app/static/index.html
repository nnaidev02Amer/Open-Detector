Methodology & limitations

What this tool does, and what it cannot

Pipeline

  1. Segment the text into paragraphs and sentences, keeping character offsets.
  2. Score each paragraph with the BERT classifier (followsci/bert-ai-text-detector), 512-token limit; long paragraphs are chunked.
  3. Window overlapping 3-sentence windows are scored to localise which sentences drive a paragraph's score.
  4. Aggregate paragraph scores by word count into the document score; bands set the verdict.

Limitations - please read

  • Not proof. The score is a stylometric signal. Human writing can look AI-like and edited or paraphrased AI text can look human-like.
  • Domain. Trained on arXiv-style academic paragraphs; accuracy drops on casual, creative or technical-manual text.
  • Fairness. Detectors of this kind are known to over-flag some non-native English writers. Treat flags with extra caution.
  • Length. Under ~100 words results are unreliable; under 40 words none is given.
  • English only. The model has no Chinese or other-language support.
  • Mixed authorship. The model cannot tell partially AI-assisted text apart; sentence highlights are estimates.

The upstream model card advises against using the output as the sole basis for any misconduct decision. This project follows that advice.
